Thinking.

Books that teach you to think again about how you think.

About this category

Thinking isn't memorizing or answering fast. It's the ability to doubt your own beliefs — and being willing to change your mind when new evidence comes in.

Books in this category teach you to escape closed mental modes — defensiveness, self-justification, identity protection — to open up to what might be more true.
