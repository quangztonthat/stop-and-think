Thinking.

Books that teach us to rethink how we think.

About this category

Thinking isn't the ability to remember a lot or answer fast. It's the ability to doubt your own beliefs — and willingness to change your mind when new evidence arrives.

Books in this category teach how to escape closed thinking modes — defensive, self-justifying, ego-protecting — to open up to what might be more true.
