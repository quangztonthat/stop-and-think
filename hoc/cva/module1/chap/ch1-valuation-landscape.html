Module 1 · Chapter 1

The Valuation Landscape

How the world went from scattered local maps to one shared navigation system for determining business value.

The Analogy

Valuation Standards Are Like GPS for Business Value

Every region once had its own maps and rules for navigating business worth. The IVSC is building one global system so that a "value reading" in Singapore means the same thing as one in New York or London.

Section 1.1

The Global Players

Like different countries building their own satellite networks, regions developed their own valuation bodies and standards over decades.

🇺🇸

North America

Led the way from the 1950s. The Appraisal Foundation created USPAP, while AICPA, ASA, NACVA and CBV Institute set credentials and practice standards.

First GPS satellites launched
🇪🇺

Europe

RICS, ICAEW, EACVA, FFEE, OIV, NIRV and CISI shaped a regionally harmonised profession, catalysed by IVS adoption and IVSC advocacy.

Regional networks joining the grid
🇸🇦

Middle East

Taqeem (est. 2012) in Saudi Arabia introduced mandatory licensing, translated IVS into Arabic, and trains valuers across the Gulf.

New coverage zones coming online
🌏

Asia Oceania

CAS in China, IVAS in Singapore, APESB in Australia — all increasingly aligning with IVS as cross-border capital flows demand consistency.

Fastest-growing signal area
Section 1.2

Four Forces Shaping the Future

These four developments are driving demand for professional business valuation — think of them as the roads that keep the GPS indispensable.

🏢

Corporate Activity

M&A, diversification, and ESG-driven acquisitions make valuation an ongoing compass for every stage of a company's life.

More drivers on the road
📊

Fair Value Reporting

IFRS 13, IFRS 3, IAS 36/38 require rigorous fair value measurement — valuers are now embedded in the financial reporting cycle.

Navigation now legally required
⚖️

Disputes & Litigation

Shareholder disputes, IP infringement, ADR — courts and arbitrators increasingly demand IVS-compliant expert opinions.

GPS evidence in court
💡

Rise of Intangibles

Brands, patents, data, software — intangible assets now dominate corporate value but sit off the balance sheet. New frameworks like IDF are emerging.

Mapping invisible terrain
Section 1.3

IVSC: The Global Standard Setter

Think of the IVSC as the international body that operates the shared satellite network — it doesn't certify individual drivers, but it ensures everyone uses the same coordinate system.

Key IVSC Milestones

1985

IVS First Published

IVSC releases the inaugural International Valuation Standards.

2014

IFRS-IVS Cooperation

IFRS Foundation and IVSC agree on protocols to harmonise fair value measurement.

2019

EU Banking Alignment

European Banking Authority publishes valuation handbook built on IVS principles.

2022

IVSC Singapore Office

IVSC's first office outside its headquarters — signalling Asia's growing valuation prominence.

2025

New IVS Edition

Latest IVS effective 31 Jan 2025, adopted in 100+ countries. Free digital version released globally.

The Big Takeaway

There is no single global standard yet — but the world is converging. IVS, adopted in 100+ countries, is the closest thing to a universal GPS. As a valuer, you must always state which standards you're using, just like a pilot states which navigation system they're flying on.
