Background: Mauboussin 25+ năm Credit Suisse Chief Investment Strategist + Columbia Business School professor. Đặc trưng: borrow lens từ ngành ngoài finance để giải bài finance.

3 framework chính: Process vs Outcome (4 quadrants — judging investor by outcome alone misleading vì luck dominates short-term); Babe Ruth Effect (frequency wrong + magnitude right = winning formula; aim for slugging not batting average); Multiple Time Horizons (different participants see different markets — edge = identify which horizon you compete on). Plus: Complexity & Phase Transitions (markets behave physically — quiet → quiet → SUDDEN; iceberg metaphor) và Mean Reversion vs Persistence (financial returns mean-revert, operational performance can persist với moats). Mình áp dụng partial: Q3/2024 acquisition deal — built process checklist 7/10 score, passed → insider sau đó took at 6/10 → 3x trong 18 tháng. Process valid nhưng VN ngắn hạn outcome luck dominate.

Mauboussin reference base = US large-cap public markets có analyst coverage thick + decades data — VN reality opposite, signal-to-noise kém

Mauboussin viết cho audience có process auditability: SEC filing standardized, analyst note công khai, conference call transcript free, Bloomberg terminal show flow. "Process focus over outcome" assume bạn có data + tools để audit process của fund manager — track record 10+ năm, factor decomposition, behavioral pattern. VN reality opposite: SME deal đa phần private, no analyst coverage, founder telling 1 story to bank, story khác to investor, story khác nữa to wife. Mauboussin's framework giả định process visible — VN: chỉ outcome visible to outsider, process invisible.

3 trục VN khác Mauboussin assume: (1) Signal-to-noise ratio kém — short-term outcome luck dominates 70-80%; cần 5-10 năm process discipline mới thấy edge. Retail VN không có patience đó. (2) Sample size insufficient — VNI mới 25 năm, <50 cycle full; Mauboussin dùng US 100+ năm data để argue mean reversion. VN chưa đủ data confirm patterns. (3) Multiple time horizons khó áp dụng — Mauboussin assume HFT/swing/long-term/multi-decade actors all present, edge từ identify which horizon. VN retail-dominated 80%, mostly swing 2-6 tháng — multi-decade actor (foreign fund, insurance) số ít. Adapt: borrow framework (process discipline, Babe Ruth slugging, complexity awareness) — but accept luck-noise dominate ngắn hạn. Track decisions journal 3-5 năm trước khi judge skill.

Skin in game Q3/2024 SME acquisition pass — mình built 3-page process checklist (industry tailwind, team quality, balance sheet, valuation, exit option), scored deal 7/10 → passed do 1 trục red. Insider sau đó took deal at 6/10 process score (mình hỏi sau, họ confirm), made 3x trong 18 tháng do consumer recovery sớm. Mauboussin lens: process valid framework — không bỏ. Outcome: 1 quyết định = sample 1 = không judge process được. Cần 20+ quyết định mới có signal. Personal stock pick 2023 VNM: mua narrative-driven (oat milk launch hype), không build process — bad process bad outcome (lawsuit + revenue miss → -22%). Bài học: process chỉ save bạn next time, outcome doesn't. 2022 short BĐS VN: built process leverage analysis chuẩn (debt/EBITDA 6x+ nhiều DN) — process right, but timing wrong (squeeze 6 tháng), exit -18%. Babe Ruth lens: didn't size for survival — short với leverage cá nhân = bị thanh lý nhanh. Lesson: process + sizing must combine — process right + size wrong = blown up trước khi thesis valid.
Framework 01 / Process vs Outcome

2x2 matrix — judge bằng process, không phải outcome

Mauboussin chương 2: "In an activity where luck plays a meaningful role, the quality of an outcome is a poor indicator of the quality of the process." Đầu tư là vậy: 4 quadrants — process tốt + outcome tốt (skill); process tốt + outcome xấu (bad luck); process xấu + outcome tốt (dumb luck); process xấu + outcome xấu (just deserts). Most people judge by outcome only → end up rewarding dumb luck and punishing bad luck → wrong incentive. Edge của Mauboussin: judge by process, accept short-term outcome variance.

Quadrant 1 · Skill
Good process + Good outcome
Repeatable. Buffett 50 năm, Soros breaking pound, Mauboussin process-driven analyst. Action: keep doing. Document process — replicate. Đây là quadrant ai cũng muốn — but rare ngắn hạn vì luck noise.
Quadrant 2 · Bad Luck
Good process + Bad outcome
Process đúng nhưng tail event xảy ra. 2022 short BĐS leverage analysis right — squeeze 6 tháng kill position. Action: keep process — repeat. Don't change strategy because of 1 bad outcome. Sample size 1 = noise.
Quadrant 3 · Dumb Luck
Bad process + Good outcome
YOLO meme stock 2021 +200% — gambler win once. SPAC retail flip. Action: don't repeat. Lucky outcome teaches wrong lesson — reinforces bad habit. Most dangerous quadrant — feels like skill but isn't.
Quadrant 4 · Just Deserts
Bad process + Bad outcome
FOMO crypto top 2022. Tin friend tip không research. Action: change process. Outcome confirms process bad. Easiest quadrant to learn from — pain forces change. Painful but useful signal.
Framework 02 / Babe Ruth Effect

Frequency wrong + magnitude right — aim for slugging, not batting average

Mauboussin chương 16: "In expected value terms, frequency does not matter — magnitude does." Babe Ruth: 1923 highest strikeout rate cùng năm highest home run rate. Lesson cho investing: VC + value investing đều high % miss + few outliers cover all. Sequoia Capital portfolio: 80% deal underperform/zero, 5% deal cover 90% returns. Don't aim for batting average (high % win) — aim for slugging (magnitude when right). Asymmetric payoff distributions thay đổi everything về sizing + holding strategy.

80 / 100 decisions
-25%
Misses. Avg loss small (capped vì position sizing). Batting average .200 = lose 80% of time. Painful psychologically — feels like failing constantly.
15 / 100 decisions
+2x
Singles. Modest wins 1.5-3x. Tổng contribution ~15% returns. Nice-to-have nhưng không change game. Most retail aim ở đây — wrong target.
5 / 100 decisions
+30x
Home runs. 10x-100x outliers. Cover 90%+ tổng returns. Đây là alpha. Sequoia: 5% deal carry portfolio. Apply: position sizing để home run có room compound (hold long, không cap upside sớm).
Framework 03 / Multiple Time Horizons

4 horizons — different actors, different signals, different games

Mauboussin chương 9: "Markets are made up of many participants with very different time horizons. The 'price' you see is the integration of all these players." Edge = identify which horizon bạn compete on, biết những actor khác đang ở horizon khác đang nhìn gì. HFT (microseconds) read order flow. Swing trader (weeks) read momentum. Long-term (years) read fundamentals. Multi-decade (10+ yrs) read regime change. Mismatch horizon = compete losing game. Retail VN thường swing 2-6 tháng nhưng dùng signal multi-decade (DCF intrinsic) → mismatch → frustrated.

HFT
microseconds · μs
Actors: Citadel, Virtu, Renaissance Tech. Co-located server. Fight for $0.0001 spread × millions trade/day.
Signal: order flow imbalance, latency arbitrage. Fundamental data IRRELEVANT — too slow. Game cá nhân không vào được.
Swing
days to weeks · 1d-3m
Actors: retail trader, momentum fund, social media follower (FB groups VN, Telegram pump). 80% volume VN ở đây.
Signal: chart pattern, news flow, sentiment shift. Fundamental MATTERS LITTLE — narrative drives. Crowded — edge khó.
Long-term
years · 1-10y
Actors: value fund, family office, patient retail. Buffett quote: "best holding period forever". 10-15% volume VN.
Signal: intrinsic value DCF, ROIC trend, moat durability. Fundamental DOMINATES. Less crowded — edge possible cho retail patient.
Multi-decade
10+ years · regime
Actors: sovereign wealth, insurance company, generational wealth (Walton, Mars). 5% volume VN — foreign fund + insurance + 1 vài family.
Signal: demographic shift, regulatory regime, geopolitical, technology super-cycle. Fundamentals annual IRRELEVANT — read 50-year arc. Least crowded — biggest edge.

Apply: identify 1 horizon you compete on + accept không compete horizon khác. Cá nhân: mình swing 5%, long-term 60%, multi-decade 35%. Skip HFT (no infrastructure) + skip swing FOMO (crowded). Edge: long-term + multi-decade ngách ít người cạnh tranh. Apply Q3/2024: passed SME deal vì swing-thinker (consumer recovery 6 tháng) — long-term horizon mình cần 5+ năm leverage runway, deal 4x leverage không qua. Insider took (swing thinker) — short term right outcome.

PART 1 · Investment Philosophy — Essays 1-10
Process over outcome. Attribute vs circumstance. Position sizing.
Foundation: how to think about investing as discipline, not gambling.

ESSAY 01
Process vs Outcome
Activity high luck → outcome poor indicator of process quality. Judge process, not result. 4 quadrants: skill, bad luck, dumb luck, just deserts. Most evaluators only look at outcome → reward dumb luck, punish bad luck. Wrong incentive structure.
Process matters
ESSAY 02
Be the House (Position Sizing)
Casino doesn't bet against single player — bets on millions of trials với edge. Investor: size positions to weather worst case, not bet on single conviction. Kelly criterion sizing: f = (bp - q) / b. Most retail over-size winners → ruin.
Sizing > conviction
ESSAY 03
Risk & Uncertainty (Knight)
Knight 1921: risk = probabilities knowable (coin flip). Uncertainty = probabilities unknowable (next pandemic). Markets mostly uncertainty, not risk. Stop using Gaussian models for fat-tailed reality. Plan for ranges, not point estimates.
Knight distinction
ESSAY 04
Are You an Expert?
Tetlock 30-year study: experts no better than chance at long-term forecast. Hedgehog (1 big idea) worse than fox (many small models). Investing: be fox — hold multiple lens, update on evidence.
Be fox not hedgehog
ESSAY 05
Right from the Get-Go
Anchoring trap: first number you see frames entire analysis. Real estate: agent ask price anchor. Stock: IPO price anchor. Calculate intrinsic FIRST, look at price SECOND. Reverse order to defeat anchor.
Anchor defense
ESSAY 06
Survival of the Fittest
Survivorship bias kills analysis. Mutual fund 30-year track record only shows survivors — failed funds delisted. "3% beat market" claim ignores 70% closed. Always ask: what's missing from my data?
Survivorship bias
ESSAY 07
Outcome Bias
Judge decision by outcome, not process at time. Same decision can have different outcomes — luck. Pre-commit decision criteria before knowing outcome. Review process quarterly — was reasoning sound regardless result?
Pre-commit criteria
ESSAY 08
Time & Patience
Compound interest = 8th wonder Einstein. Time horizon = competitive advantage. Most investor 12-month focus → opportunity for 10-year focus. Buffett: "time is the friend of wonderful business, enemy of mediocre".
Time arbitrage
ESSAY 09
Multiple Time Horizons
Different participants different horizons → different "markets" same price. Edge = identify your horizon + accept không compete khác. HFT vs swing vs long vs multi-decade — pick lane. VN retail mostly swing — crowded.
Pick horizon
ESSAY 10
Capital Allocation = CEO's Job
"In a properly run business, capital allocation decisions are the chairman's most important job." 5 channels: reinvest, M&A, dividend, buyback, debt repay. Most CEO bad at this — promoted from operations, not finance. Look for capital-allocator CEO.
CEO allocation
PART 2 · Psychology of Investing — Essays 11-19
Babe Ruth effect. Multiple promotions. Emotion management.
How brain breaks during investing — and how to compensate.
← Trượt ngang để xem →
ESSAY 11
Babe Ruth Effect
Ruth 1923: highest strikeout + highest home run. Lesson: frequency wrong + magnitude right = winning. Sequoia: 5% deal cover 90% returns. Aim slugging not batting average. Position size để home run compound — không cap upside sớm.
Slugging
ESSAY 12
Sorting Sheep from Goats
Manager evaluation: how distinguish skill from luck? Mauboussin's test: process consistency + diverse outcomes. Skill = stable process across regimes. Luck = lucky in 1 regime, fail in next. Need 5+ years multiple regimes data.
Skill test
ESSAY 13
Right Tail of the Distribution
Most investing returns concentrated in few outliers. Right tail = where money made. Strategy: many small bets to get exposure to right tail, sized so left tail (losers) don't kill you. Asymmetric payoff design.
Right tail focus
ESSAY 14
Multiple Promotions Bias
Why 80% mutual fund underperform? Career risk: manager promoted by short-term result → forced index hugging → can't outperform. Independent capital (founder, family office, retail) has structural advantage — no career risk.
Career risk trap
ESSAY 15
Information Cascades
When everyone defers to "what others know" — information cascade forms. Stock bubbles work this way. Independent thinker breaks cascade. Pre-commit position before reading consensus. Read consensus AFTER decision.
Independent thinking
ESSAY 16
Emotion & Decision
Damasio neuroscience: emotion BUILT INTO decision-making — patients with damaged emotion brain can't decide. Goal: not eliminate emotion — manage it. Pre-commit rules so emotion can't override at moment of stress.
Manage emotion
ESSAY 17
Stress & Performance
Inverted-U: low stress = under-perform, medium = peak, high = collapse. Most investors operate in high-stress zone (leverage, concentrated, monitoring daily). Reduce stress to medium → better decisions. Cash buffer + position rules → reduce stress.
Inverted-U
ESSAY 18
Wisdom of Crowds (and Folly)
Galton 1906 ox weight: crowd average accurate. WHEN: independent + diverse + decentralized. WHEN crowd fails: information cascade + herding → wrong consensus. Markets oscillate between wisdom and folly. Read which mode you're in.
Wisdom vs folly
ESSAY 19
Mind on Money
Why so many smart people fail at investing? Smart in 1 domain ≠ smart across domains. Doctor good at medicine = bad at investing (different feedback loop, different uncertainty). Domain expertise non-transferable. Stay humble outside circle of competence.
Domain humility
PART 3 · Innovation & Competitive Strategy — Essays 20-29
Creative destruction. Network effects. Disruption patterns.
How industries change, why moats erode, where competitive advantage lives.
← Trượt ngang để xem →
ESSAY 20
Creative Destruction
Schumpeter: capitalism destroys to create. S&P 500 turnover accelerating: 1958 avg tenure 61 năm; 2024 ~15 năm. No moat permanent. Buy & hold forever = wrong assumption. Reassess moat every 3-5 năm.
Moat erosion
ESSAY 21
Network Effects
Metcalfe's Law: value = N². Facebook, Visa, Microsoft Office. Network effect = winner-take-most. Disrupt requires switching cost crash. VN: Zalo dominate via SMS replacement; VietQR dominate via standardization. Identify network monopoly early.
N²
ESSAY 22
Innovator's Dilemma (Christensen)
Big company can't disrupt itself — disruption from below at lower margin. Listen to wrong customer = death. Kodak, Blockbuster, Nokia. Apply: incumbent dominant in 1 segment is fragile to lower-tier entrant going up-market.
Disruption from below
ESSAY 23
Strategic Power Sources
Hamilton Helmer 7 Powers: scale economy, network, counter-positioning, switching cost, brand, cornered resource, process power. Real moat = 1+ Power, not just "great brand". Audit your portfolio: which Power does each holding have?
7 Powers
ESSAY 24
Business Cycle Phases
4 phases: early (recovery, low capacity), mid (growth, full capacity), late (overheating, leverage), recession. Sector rotation by phase: early = cyclical, mid = growth, late = defensive, recession = quality survivor. VN Q4/2024 = late phase signals.
4 phases
ESSAY 25
Diffusion of Innovation
S-curve adoption: innovator 2.5%, early adopter 13.5%, early majority 34%, late majority 34%, laggard 16%. Investment timing: enter at chasm crossing (15-20% adoption) — risk lower, upside still 5-10x. EV in VN ~5% — pre-chasm.
S-curve timing
ESSAY 26
Increasing Returns
Industrial economics: diminishing returns. Information economics: increasing returns (more users → more value → more users). Software, platform, marketplace. Different valuation framework needed — DCF doesn't capture network compounding.
Increasing returns
ESSAY 27
Long Tail
Anderson 2004: digital reduce inventory cost → niche viable. Aggregate of niches = 50%+ of market. Amazon books, Spotify songs, Netflix shows. Investment: aggregator of long tail (platform) more durable than head (single hit).
Long tail
ESSAY 28
Power Laws (Pareto)
Distribution non-Gaussian: 80/20 rule everywhere. Top 1% companies generate 80% market value. VN: top 30 stocks ~80% market cap. Implication: index concentrated → diversification myth. Active select top 30 = different game.
Power law
ESSAY 29
Mean Reversion vs Persistence
Financial returns mean-revert (eventually). Operational performance can persist với moats. Visa ROIC 30% năm 20 năm — persistence. Generic retailer 10% → 8% → 6% — reversion. Knowing which framework apply when = critical edge.
Reversion vs persistence
PART 4 · Science & Complexity — Essays 30-38
Phase transitions. Iceberg metaphor. Fitness landscape. Adaptation.
Markets behave like complex physical systems — not equilibrium.
← Trượt ngang để xem →
ESSAY 30
Phase Transitions
Water → ice at 0°C. Markets behave physically — quiet → quiet → quiet → SUDDEN. Linear extrapolation fails at phase boundaries. 2008 looked stable until wasn't. 2020 COVID similar. Apply: stop assuming smooth — prepare for discontinuity.
Discontinuity
ESSAY 31
Iceberg Metaphor
Visible 10% above water — 90% below. Market price = surface; underlying credit, leverage, sentiment = iceberg below. 2007 mortgage AAA looked safe — leverage 30:1 below water. Read what's NOT visible.
Below water
ESSAY 32
Fitness Landscape
Biology: species adapt to local fitness peak. Stuck on local peak = can't reach higher peak across valley. Companies same — local optimum trap. Kodak film peak → couldn't cross to digital valley. Apply: incumbent stuck = fragile to nimble newcomer.
Local peak trap
ESSAY 33
Adaptation Failure
Why smart organizations fail to adapt? Success makes inflexible. Past success = current process = future failure. GE jet engine, Sears retail, Boeing 737-MAX. Apply: avoid "best-in-class" overpriced — past success = lagging indicator.
Past success trap
ESSAY 34
Stocks & Bonds Long-term
Siegel data 200 years: stock 6.5% real, bond 3.5% real, cash 0.5% real. Stock dominates over 30+ years — but with -50% drawdowns. Asset allocation = optimize utility, not return. Most retail need 60/40 not 100/0.
200-year view
ESSAY 35
Risk-Reward in Practice
Theoretical Sharpe ratio assumes Gaussian. Real distributions fat-tailed. LTCM blew up because Gaussian model. Apply: stress test với 4-5 sigma, not 2 sigma. -25% scenario should fit budget — not just -10%.
Fat tail stress
ESSAY 36
Volatility & Information
Volatility ≠ noise — sometimes information. Spike in volatility = regime change signal. VIX >30 historically marker of fear extreme = buy opportunity (probabilistic). Don't ignore vol — read it.
Vol as signal
ESSAY 37
Diversification Limits
10-15 stocks already 80% diversification benefit. 30+ stocks = closet indexing with cost. Concentrated 8-12 best ideas = optimal balance. Mauboussin reference: focus + size = where edge lives. Over-diversification kill returns.
Concentrated focus
ESSAY 38
Pulling It All Together
Synthesis: "Investing is not a science but a craft. Multiple lenses, accumulated wisdom, process discipline." 38 essays don't collapse to 1 rule — they're a toolkit. Apply lens that fits situation. Multidisciplinary breadth = real edge.
Multi-lens toolkit
