The Most Important Thing.

Đầu tư thành công không phải doing the right thing — mà avoiding the wrong thing. Edge của thoughtful investor: think differently và better.

Marks viết The Most Important Thing như essay collection — condense 20+ năm memo Oaktree thành 21 "most important things". Background: Marks cofounded Oaktree Capital 1995, specialty distressed debt + credit cycle. Đầu tư style: defensive first, focus avoid loss thay vì max gain. Cốt lõi: không có 1 thing duy nhất — plural — vì market complex, mỗi tình huống cần lens khác.

3 framework chính: Second-Level Thinking (first-level: "company tốt → buy"; second-level: "everyone đã think tốt → priced in → not opportunity"); Pendulum (markets không equilibrium, swing greed ↔ fear extreme — buy at fear extreme, sell at greed extreme); Risk = Permanent Loss (KHÔNG phải volatility; β meaningless; risk chỉ realize được retrospective). Plus: Defensive Investing (compound math: -50% cần +100% recover) và Cycles + Reflexivity (credit cycle drive everything). Mình áp dụng partial: 2018-2021 hold cash chờ "blood" → cost of patience cao; 2022 deploy at -25% → +18% recover; Q4/2024 pass SME deal vì late cycle.

"
Investing is a popularity contest, and the most dangerous thing is to buy something at the peak of its popularity.
Howard Marks, The Most Important Thing

Marks viết cho institutional capital chịu được 5-10 năm illiquid + 50% drawdown — retail VN không có structural advantage đó

Marks Oaktree là credit-distressed expert. Style "buy when blood in streets" assume 3 thứ: (1) bạn có cash để deploy đúng lúc blood, (2) employer KHÔNG lay off bạn cùng crisis, (3) family KHÔNG cần tiền cùng lúc. 2008-2009 retail Mỹ mass redeemed at bottom — không phải foolish, đó là survival. Marks 2009 deploy billions, retail Mỹ panic sold IRA → both rational at their structural level. Edge của Marks không phải insight — mà structural advantage: lockup capital 5-10 năm, leverage cheap, team analyst 50+ người, phone call CEOs. Retail VN không có 1/10 trong số đó.

3 trục VN khác Marks assume: (1) Liquidity asymmetry — cash bạn cần khi crisis (mất việc, family cần) thường trùng đáy thị trường; (2) No cheap leverage — Marks dùng đòn bẩy cost 3-5%, retail VN margin 12-15% không hold qua drawdown 12 tháng được; (3) Information asymmetry — Marks call CEO biết true balance sheet, retail VN read báo cáo công khai sau 6 tháng. Adapt Marks: borrow framework (second-level thinking, defensive math, asymmetric recovery) — but not the timing edge. DCA đều đặn + cash buffer 12 tháng spend + chỉ deploy khi drawdown >25% với portion nhỏ <30% NAV mỗi lần.

Skin in game 2018-2021 mình hold 50% cash chờ "blood" theo Marks framework — Marks viết 2017-2018 memo cảnh báo "the seven worst words: too much money chasing too few deals". Thực tế: VNI bull tiếp 3 năm, mình missed ~80% gain. Cost of patience: nếu 50% cash đó DCA index sẽ +60-80%, mình kiếm 4-5%. Bài học: Marks's call macro đúng (cycle late) nhưng timing wrong 3 năm — institutional có thể chịu 3 năm "look stupid", retail thì capital lock idle costly. 2022 crash mình deploy 50% cash buffer at -25% drawdown → +18% recover trong 18 tháng. Marks framework worked — KHI cuối cùng wave xảy ra. Q4/2024 pass SME deal IRR 18% vì cycle position late + balance sheet 4x leverage — Marks lens: late cycle = expensive risk dù pricing đẹp.
Framework 01 / Second-Level Thinking

First-level vs Second-level — 5 stock examples

Marks chương 1: "Your goal isn't to earn average returns; you want to do better than average." First-level thinking dễ — ai cũng làm được — nên không tạo edge. Second-level thinking: think về việc người khác đang nghĩ gì, đã price in chưa, và bạn nghĩ sai chỗ nào. Edge = thinking what others missed. Nếu cùng kết luận với consensus → consensus đã price in → no opportunity.

Level 1 · Surface · Consensus
Level 2 · Deep · What's priced in?
Apple · 2024
"Apple là công ty tuyệt vời, brand mạnh nhất thế giới, services growing. Buy."
Apple · L2
"Apple tuyệt — nhưng MỌI người biết Apple tuyệt. P/E 30x đã price in growth 8-10%/năm 5 năm tới. Nếu growth chỉ 5% → multiple compress → -30%. Câu hỏi đúng: điều gì market chưa biết?"
Tesla · 2021
"Tesla disrupting automotive + energy + AI. EV growth massive. Musk visionary. Buy."
Tesla · L2
"Tesla narrative đã consensus. P/E 1000x discount 20+ năm growth. Nếu BYD + legacy auto compete được — multiple collapse 80%. Risk = price-of-perfection. Skip dù story đúng."
VNM · 2018
"Vinamilk dominant 50% market share, brand mạnh, dividend 6%. Buy hold forever."
VNM · L2
"Dominance đã price in P/E 22x — discount mature growth 8%. Nhưng plant-based + competitor TH True Milk + dairy import tariff cut sắp xảy ra. Margin contract → growth 4%. Đáy thực 2022 -45%. L2 sell 2018, buy lại 2022."
HPG · 2020
"Hòa Phát tăng trưởng thép, capacity expand Dung Quất 2. EBITDA tăng. Buy."
HPG · L2
"Cyclical at peak. P/E thấp = peak earnings, không cheap. Steel price $700/tấn = 10-year high. Mean revert → margin halve → EPS halve → P/E 12x → 24x at lower price. L2: avoid peak cyclical. Trip 2022 thép $400/tấn → -55%."
ACB · 2022 (-50%)
"Bank crisis Vạn Thịnh Phát + interest rate spike. Bond market frozen. Sell — risk too high."
ACB · L2
"Vạn Thịnh Phát risk = SCB-specific, KHÔNG ACB. Market panic indiscriminate. ACB P/B 0.9, ROE 22%, NPL 0.7%. Discount cho risk không exist. L2: max courage buy NOW khi mọi người sell. +85% trong 14 tháng."
Framework 02 / Pendulum Swing

Markets không equilibrium — swing giữa greed và fear

Marks chương 9: "The mood swings of the securities markets resemble the movement of a pendulum." Pendulum dành rất ít thời gian ở midpoint — chủ yếu swing giữa 2 extreme: euphoria-depression, optimism-pessimism, risk tolerance-risk aversion. Buy when pendulum at fear extreme. Sell when at greed extreme. Vấn đề: most painful moment = best entry. Most comfortable moment = worst entry. Counter-intuitive nên ít người làm.

1999 · Greed peak
Dotcom euphoria
Pets.com IPO $400M no revenue. P/E sectors 200x+. "This time is different". Marks 2000 memo bubble.com — sold heavy. Crash -78% NASDAQ 2 năm tiếp.
2009 · Fear peak
GFC trough
Bank insolvency, retail panic. S&P 666. Distressed credit yield 20%+. Marks Oaktree deploy $25B trong 2008-09. +88% S&P 12 tháng tiếp.
2021 · Greed peak
COVID rally
SPAC mania, meme stocks (GME, AMC), crypto $3T, ARK 200x. "Stocks always go up". VNI 1500. Marks Q4/21 memo cảnh báo. -50% NASDAQ + -34% VNI 2022.
2022 · Fear peak
Trough · buy
VNI 900 (-40%), bank ACB P/B 0.9, real estate -60%. Vạn Thịnh Phát + bond market frozen. Mình deploy 50% cash Q4/22. +50% VNI 18 tháng tiếp. Painful entry.
Framework 03 / Asymmetric Loss Math

-50% cần +100% recover — recovery math KHÔNG symmetric

Marks chương 7 + 17: "Avoiding losses is more important than scoring big wins." Compound math asymmetric — loss hơn gain. Mất 10% cần +11% recover. Mất 50% cần +100%. Mất 75% cần +300%. Defensive investing = focus avoid -50%, không max +100%. Vì +100% sau -50% = chỉ về break-even, KHÔNG win. 2 nhà đầu tư: A đều +12%/năm 10 năm = 3.1x. B đều +20% 9 năm + 1 năm -50% = 2.6x. Steady wins.

Apply: tránh -50% drawdown important hơn bắt +100% upside. Position sizing rule cá nhân: không position nào >15% NAV, không leverage >1.3x, exit khi -25% individual position (stop loss hard). Mình personal: 2018 hold 30% single stock real estate → -45% Q1/2020 COVID → mất 24 tháng recover, opportunity cost cực cao. Sau 2020 enforce 15% rule strict.

21 most important things — condensed 4 parts

Bản đồ The Most Important Thing

Marks viết 21 chương = 21 "most important things". Plural — không có 1 thing duy nhất. Mình condense thành 4 PHẦN theo arc thực hành: Foundations (think differently, market efficiency, value); Risk (understand, recognize, control, avoid loss); Cycles (cycle awareness, pendulum, contrarian, bargains); Discipline (knowing what you don't know, defense, patience, expectations).

PART 1 · Foundations — Things 1-4
Think differently. Value over price. Edge từ asymmetric insight.
Second-level thinking. Market (in)efficiency. Value. Price-value gap.
THING 01
Second-Level Thinking
First-level: "company tốt → buy". Second-level: "everyone biết tốt → priced in → sell". Edge = thinking what consensus missed. Nếu cùng kết luận với consensus → no opportunity. Marks: "Your goal isn't average returns — you want better than average. Thinking phải different + higher level."
Edge
THING 02
Understanding Market (In)Efficiency
EMH true cho large-cap US — không cho mọi market. Inefficient markets = where edge possible: small-cap, distressed, emerging market, illiquid. Marks: "Tôi không waste time picking S&P stocks — index. Active money goes vào credit distressed nơi inefficient."
Inefficiency edge
THING 03
Value
2 schools: value (cheap vs intrinsic) và growth (future cash flow discount). Marks value-school. Discipline: establish intrinsic value INDEPENDENT của price. Mua khi market price << intrinsic. Sell khi price >> intrinsic. "It's not what you buy, it's what you pay."
Intrinsic value
THING 04
Price & Value Relationship
Same asset có thể bargain hoặc rip-off — tùy price tại thời điểm mua. Apple $90 = bargain, $300 = rip-off. "No asset is so good it can't become a bad investment if bought too high. Few assets so bad they can't be a good investment when bought low enough."
Price matters
PART 2 · Risk — Things 5-7
Risk = permanent loss, NOT volatility — define, recognize, control
Understand risk. Recognize risk. Control risk.
THING 05
Understanding Risk
Academic finance: risk = volatility = β. Marks: RISK = PROBABILITY OF PERMANENT LOSS. Volatility ≠ risk — bond zigzag không matter nếu hold đến maturity. Risk là khả năng KHÔNG recover được. Β meaningless cho long-term investor. "Risk only realized in retrospect — until rare event happens, looks safe."
Permanent loss
THING 06
Recognizing Risk
Risk highest khi mọi người nghĩ không có risk. Counter-intuitive: perceived safety = real danger. 2007 mortgage AAA — perceived risk-free → priced for perfection → real risk highest. 2009 distressed bond yield 20% — perceived risky → priced for disaster → real risk LOW.
Perception flip
THING 07
Controlling Risk
Không thể eliminate risk — chỉ control. Methods: (1) position size (no single position >15%), (2) diversification (across asset class + cycle), (3) margin of safety (buy 30%+ below intrinsic), (4) avoid leverage ở late cycle. Marks Oaktree slogan: "If we avoid the losers, the winners take care of themselves."
Risk control
PART 3 · Cycles — Things 8-12
Cycles + pendulum + contrarian — buy fear, sell greed
Cycles. Pendulum. Negative influences. Contrarian. Bargains.
THING 08
Being Attentive to Cycles
Markets don't go in straight lines — credit cycle drives everything. Easy money → bad lending → losses → tight money → no liquidity → forced sale → bargains → repeat. Marks's mantra: "We may never know where we're going, but we'd better have a good idea where we are."
Where are we?
THING 09
Awareness of Pendulum
Markets không equilibrium — pendulum swing greed ↔ fear, hiếm khi midpoint. Buy at fear extreme, sell at greed extreme. Most painful = best entry. 1999/2009/2021/2022 all extreme. Reading sentiment = reading pendulum position.
Pendulum
THING 10
Combating Negative Influences
Psychology destroys investors: greed, fear, herd, ego, capitulation. Most lethal: capitulation at extremes (sell bottom, buy top). Defense: written investment plan, position rules, automatic rebalance. Đừng trust intuition khi pendulum extreme — intuition ép bạn herd.
Anti-herd
THING 11
Contrarianism
"To be a successful contrarian, you have to be willing to look wrong for a long time." Contrarian ≠ always opposite — contrarian = independent thinker. Buy at bottom requires courage when consensus says doom. Sell at top requires discipline when consensus says nothing can stop. Marks: 2007 sold heavy, looked stupid 1 year. Recovered.
Look wrong long
THING 12
Finding Bargains
Bargain characteristics: little known, fundamentally questionable on surface, controversial/unappealing, declining, stigmatized. Things mà institutional investor CAN'T buy (mandate, size, reputation) → that's where bargains live. Distressed bonds, frontier markets, fallen angels.
Where institutions can't go
PART 4 · Discipline — Things 13-21
Patience + humility + defense + reasonable expectation
Patient opportunism. Knowing what you don't know. Where we stand. Luck. Defense. Pitfalls. Adding value. Reasonable expectations. Pulling all together.
THING 13
Patient Opportunism
Don't search for opportunity — wait for it to come. Best investments khi market hands them to you (panic). Most of the time, do nothing. Patience = competitive edge vì most investor can't sit on cash 12-24 tháng. Marks: "Wait for the pitch."
Wait for pitch
THING 14
Knowing What You Don't Know
2 schools: "I know" (forecast macro, time market) — most popular, most wrong. "I don't know" (humble, focus what's controllable). Marks "I don't know" school. Don't predict — prepare for range of outcomes. Sizing position to survive worst case.
Humility
THING 15
Sense for Where We Stand
Can't predict — but can READ current temperature. Marks's checklist: credit easy/tight? Pundits bullish/bearish? IPOs hot/cold? Risk premium high/low? Position sizing reflects temperature: aggressive when fear, defensive when greed. Not market timing — risk calibration.
Read temperature
THING 16
Appreciating Role of Luck
Result = skill + luck. Single year: mostly luck. 10+ years: skill emerges. Don't conflate good outcome with good decision. Could've been right for wrong reason. Process > outcome. Same decision can produce different outcomes — judge by process quality, not result.
Process > outcome
THING 17
Investing Defensively
2 ways to win: (1) score lots of points (offense), (2) prevent opponent scoring (defense). Marks pick defense. Avoid -50% drawdown more important than chase +100% upside. Compound math asymmetric. Steady -5% to +20% beats volatile -50% to +60% over decades.
Defense first
THING 18
Avoiding Pitfalls
Pitfalls Marks list: imagination failure (didn't think it could happen), over-reliance on history (this time IS different sometimes), over-extending at peak, wishful thinking override evidence. Defense: pre-mortem (imagine failure scenarios), diversify, never go all-in single thesis.
Pre-mortem
THING 19
Adding Value
Asymmetric value: outperform in bad times more important than great times. Up 100% in good year + down 50% in bad year = lose to 0% all years. Marks Oaktree's track record: capture 60% of upside, only 30% of downside. Asymmetric capture = real alpha.
Asymmetric capture
THING 20
Reasonable Expectations
Most error: expect too much. Stock historical 8-10%/year long-term. Anyone promise 20%+ consistently — lying or lucky soon to revert. Set expectation 7-9% real return. Anything above = bonus from cycle timing or skill (likely temporary).
7-9% expectation
THING 21
Pulling It All Together
Marks's synthesis: "Most successful investors don't have the smartest IQs — they have the temperament + discipline to apply boring framework consistently". 21 things không cần memorize — cần internalize 5: think second-level, value matters, risk = permanent loss, cycle awareness, defense first.
Synthesis
